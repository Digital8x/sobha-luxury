Sobha Solis — Key Information

Sobha Solis is strategically located in Motor City, Dubai — offering quiet parkland surroundings, premium sports facilities, and quick connectivity to Al Qudra Road, Hessa Street, and Downtown Dubai.

Sobha Solis features premium 1, 1.5, 2, and 3-bedroom luxury apartment residences with spacious private balconies, acoustic insulation, and floor-to-ceiling glass facades.

Starting prices for Sobha Solis residences begin from AED 2.14M*. Detailed residence floor plans with specific unit pricing and allocations are available upon request.

Handover is anticipated for December 2027. The project features an investor-friendly 60:40 construction-linked payment plan with structured installments during build and 40% on completion.

Residents enjoy an exclusive Arsenal-branded fitness zone, resort lagoon pools, padel tennis courts, wellness spas, landscaped jogging tracks, and 24/7 private concierge security.
