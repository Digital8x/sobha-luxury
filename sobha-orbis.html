About Sobha Orbis

A Self-Contained Urban Sanctuary

Sobha Orbis is a masterfully planned seven-tower residential enclave set within Dubai's vibrant Motor City district. Positioned to redefine modern urban living, the towers are interconnected by expansive landscaped podiums, resort lagoons, and private lifestyle amenities.

Each residence is crafted with Sobha's hallmark backward-integrated construction quality, featuring floor-to-ceiling glass facades, German sanitary fittings, European kitchen appliances, and panoramic skyline vistas.

Sobha Orbis — Frequently Asked Questions

Sobha Orbis is located in Motor City, Dubai. It is a multi-tower development comprising seven residential towers with rapid highway connectivity via Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road.

The development offers 1, 1.5, and 2-bedroom luxury apartments. The 1.5-bedroom units include a dedicated study room, ideal for modern working professionals.

The indexed official listing indicates a starting reference price of AED 1.70 million. Contact our advisory team for real-time inventory and pricing for specific floors and layouts.

Handover is scheduled for December 2027. Construction milestones are regularly published and verified under Dubai Land Department (RERA) oversight.

Sobha Orbis features expansive resort pools, fitness suites, landscaped sky gardens, ground-floor dining and retail, children's playgrounds, and round-the-clock concierge services.

Residences feature an investor-friendly 20:80 payment structure with staged construction-linked milestones. Request the full schedule from our sales advisors.
