DIGITAL TRANSPARENCY & COMPLIANCE

Cookie Notice & Tracking Policy

Effective Date: October 2026  |  Version 2.1  |  Applicable to Sobha Luxury UAE Portals

01. What Are Cookies & Why We Use Them

Cookies are compact text files stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile handset when you navigate web portals. They allow our systems to recognize your browser, maintain session security, ensure high-fidelity rendering of architectural media, and save your preferred residence choices as you explore our developments.

02. Categories of Cookies Deployed

Our digital platforms implement four defined tiers of cookies to serve distinct functionality:

  • Essential Operational Cookies: Fundamental files strictly required for network routing, security protocol enforcement, bot prevention, and modal display states. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference & Localization Cookies: Remember your selected international country dial-code, currency choices, and development interest contexts across page transitions.
  • Aggregated Analytics Cookies: Enable our engineering team to assess website performance, identify popular residence layouts, and optimize load speeds without storing directly identifiable personal identities.
  • Attribution & Campaign Cookies: Preserve campaign tags (e.g. UTM parameters) so our senior client concierge team understands which verified digital campaign or invitation introduced you to Sobha.

03. Managing & Disabling Cookies

Visitors maintain full control over cookie storage. You can freely adjust your browser settings to reject non-essential cookies, clear existing cookies, or notify you when a cookie is transmitted:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Please note that disabling strictly essential cookies may affect interactive features such as the private consultation modal and floor plan download requests.
