About Sobha Siniya Island

A Different Perspective on Luxury

Sobha Siniya Island is a sanctuary of rare tranquility, situated off the coast of Umm Al Quwain and connected by an iconic causeway to the UAE mainland. Embracing centuries of coastal heritage, turquoise lagoons, and lush mangrove reserves, this master island destination brings an international level of resort residential luxury to the Northern Emirates.

Discerning residents enjoy private beach access, direct boat moorings, championship golf course vistas, and a private yacht marina club — all within a 50-minute scenic drive from Downtown Dubai.

The island presents two premier villa collections: the verdant Palm Grove Villas and the absolute waterfront Coral Beach Villas.
