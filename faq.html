Ownership & Legal Framework

Yes. Foreign nationals of any nationality can purchase and own 100% freehold title deeds in designated investment zones across Dubai and the UAE. All Sobha developments featured on this platform — including Sobha Solis, Sobha Orbis, Sobha Central, Sobha Sanctuary, and Sobha Siniya Island — are in approved freehold locations, giving buyers full legal ownership rights to sell, lease, mortgage, or pass down through inheritance.

Under current UAE immigration regulations, real estate investors purchasing properties valued at AED 2,000,000 (approximately USD 545,000) or more are eligible to apply for the prestigious 10-Year Renewable Golden Visa. Key benefits include full family sponsorship (spouse and children of any age), domestic staff sponsorship, zero restriction on duration spent outside the UAE, and residency renewable every 10 years as long as property ownership is retained.

Payment Plans & Escrow Protections

Dubai and the UAE enforce some of the strictest real estate regulatory frameworks globally under the Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA). All buyer payments are deposited directly into a designated government-supervised project Escrow Account with an authorized bank. Funds can only be released to the developer in stages as certified construction milestones are verified by independent RERA site inspectors.

Sobha Realty offers flexible developer-direct milestone payment plans:

• Sobha Solis: Standard 60:40 plan (60% during construction linked to milestones, 40% on completion).
• Sobha Central: 60:40 construction-linked plan with December 2029 handover.
• Sobha Orbis: 80:20 standard plan, with limited-time 20:80 promotions subject to current circular approval.
• Sobha Sanctuary & Siniya Island: Bespoke milestone schedules with promotional offers confirmed per villa reservation.

Standard regulatory purchase costs in Dubai include:

• Dubai Land Department (DLD) Transfer Fee: 4% of the purchase price + admin fees.
• Oqood / Pre-Registration Fee: Approximately AED 1,000–3,000.
• Developer Admin Fee: Typically AED 5,000 (inclusive of VAT).
• Agency Commission: 0% when purchasing developer-direct through our advisory team.

Backward Integration & Engineering Quality

Sobha Realty operates on a unique model of complete Backward Integration. Unlike conventional developers who outsource to external general contractors, Sobha controls 100% of the value chain in-house: architectural conceptualization, structural engineering, facade engineering, joinery, glazing, metal manufacturing, and landscaping. This German-inspired standard of engineering ensures unmatched precision, absence of construction snags, and on-time project handovers.

Sobha conducts rigorous pre-handover snagging and provides a comprehensive 1-year defect liability warranty for all fixtures, MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing), and finishes, alongside a mandatory 10-year structural warranty backed by UAE law.

Dubai prime residential developments traditionally yield between 6% to 9% gross annual rental income, significantly outpacing global metropolitan capitals like London, New York, or Singapore. In addition, residential rental yields in Dubai enjoy a 0% personal income tax and 0% capital gains tax environment.
