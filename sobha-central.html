About Sobha Central

An Integrated City Landmark

Sobha Central is an ambitious mixed-use vertical master community prominently situated along Sheikh Zayed Road, adjacent to the thriving Jumeirah Lake Towers and Dubai Marina districts. Conceived as a multi-dimensional urban enclave, it unites six architectural towers with elevated sky parks, Grade-A creative workspaces, and a bustling dining and retail promenade.

Residences are distinguished by three distinct interior collections — The Eden, The Tranquil, and The Horizon — each engineered with acoustic sound-insulating glass facades, smart automation, and bespoke joinery handcrafted by Sobha's in-house craftsmen.

Sobha Central — Questions & Answers

Sobha Central is situated on Sheikh Zayed Road in Dubai, adjacent to the Jumeirah Lake Towers district. Its prime highway location provides immediate access to Dubai's principal business and leisure hubs.

The project offers one- and two-bedroom luxury apartments across three distinct collections: The Eden, The Tranquil, and The Horizon. Contact our team to confirm current availability by tower and floor level.

The published starting prices begin at AED 1.83 million for The Eden, AED 1.85 million for The Tranquil, and AED 1.91 million for The Horizon, subject to inventory availability.

The master development has an anticipated handover reference of December 2029, regulated under official RERA construction milestones.

Amenities include elevated green sky parks, infinity swimming pools, integrated retail and gourmet dining promenades, executive coworking suites, and 24/7 security and concierge services.
